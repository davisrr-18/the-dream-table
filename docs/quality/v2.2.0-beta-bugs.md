# The Dream Table 2.2.0-beta Bug Ledger

Known findings in flows changed for 2.2 are recorded here through release validation. Resolved findings remain as regression history.

## Resolved

### Repair overlap check could remove a repairing farm

- Severity: High
- Status: Resolved
- Reproduction: Start Repair on a finished farm with fewer than half of its blocks standing, then attempt an overlapping build from another table.
- Expected: The overlapping build is refused and the repairing farm keeps its identity, name, grants, alerts, and persisted record.
- Actual: The conflict survey could remove the FINISHED record before finding the active build reservation, leaving the Repair lease without its farm record.
- Cause: The forgotten-farm check treated a leased FINISHED farm as removable when its physical presence was below the normal threshold.
- Regression coverage: `FinishedFarmsTest` keeps the leased record through a conflict check and persistence round trip.
- Resolution: A FINISHED record with an active Repair lease is never forgotten by conflict detection.

### Alert rendering could hide or fail to acknowledge alerts

- Severity: Medium
- Status: Resolved
- Reproduction: Accept an invitation, open a farm alert page, then render it repeatedly or resume a previously blocked Repair.
- Expected: Active problems are visible to every authorized collaborator, post-acceptance history is visible, acknowledgements succeed, and resolved Repair problems leave the active list.
- Actual: Alert timestamps used world ticks while grants used Unix milliseconds; every render sent a new request that superseded the displayed token; Repair warnings stayed active after recovery.
- Cause: Alert time, request lifetime, and Repair-resolution state were not aligned with access and page-token contracts.
- Regression coverage: `FarmAlertViewTest`, `FarmAlertsTest`, `FarmAlertPageTokensTest`, `FinishedFarmsTest`, and `LoadedFarmsViewTest` cover time/privacy, page state, persistence, and the detail refresh gate.
- Resolution: Alerts use Unix milliseconds, active events bypass the history cutoff, page requests are gated per selection, and Repair recovery resolves prior Repair alerts.

### Repair can leave a minecart passenger unmounted

- Severity: Medium
- Status: Resolved
- Reproduction: Damage an iron farm so only its zombie passenger or only its empty vanilla minecart remains, then start Repair. A failed entity spawn or mount also reproduces an orphaned partial pair.
- Expected: Repair reuses exactly one free matching passenger or one empty vanilla minecart in that cell, creates only the missing component, and rolls back only entities created by the failed attempt.
- Actual: Repair treated any matching passenger as complete. When spawning a pair, it ignored a failed mount and could leave newly spawned entities behind after a partial failure.
- Cause: Entity presence was checked independently of the required passenger/cart relationship, and the spawn path did not model partial states or verify `startRiding`.
- Regression coverage: `MinecartPairingTest` covers the complete partial-state matrix, exact cell filtering, ambiguous candidates, and mount-failure rollback; `BuildIssueTest` covers the persisted non-temporary entity blockage.
- Resolution: Exact local counts now choose one explicit pairing action. Occupied or ambiguous cells stop with `ENTITY_BLOCKED`; spawn and mount failures discard only entities created by that call.
